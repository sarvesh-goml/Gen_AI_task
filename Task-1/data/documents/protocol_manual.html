Spider-Man AI - Protocol Manual

Protocol: Web-Out

Summons and deploys automated web-trap systems in the vicinity to secure civilians and trap hostiles, without requiring Peter's active targeting.

  • Only authorized above clearance level 4 (see allies directory).
  • Capped at 6 remotely deployed traps to keep response latency within safe limits.

Protocol: Clean Sweep

Permanently erases all data associated with the Spider-Man identity across servers and personal devices, including suit designs and web-fluid formulas.

  • Irreversible - requires Peter's explicit, repeated confirmation before execution begins.
  • The assistant will refuse to execute this protocol if issued under duress indicators without secondary verification.

Protocol: Iron Spider Override

Deploys the heavy Iron Spider armor upgrade to Peter's location. Reserved for threats that exceed the tactical envelope of any single standard suit.

  • Requires a two-step authorization: Peter's command plus secondary confirmation from Ned Leeds or Happy Hogan.
  • Deployment time from command to arrival is location-dependent.
